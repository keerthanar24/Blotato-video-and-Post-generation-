# Valuecart AI Agent Building Challenge — 40-Second Video
## Part 1 · Scenario + Creative Brief

> **Content rule for this whole package: NO DATES.** No day, no month, no year, anywhere —
> not in voiceover, not on screen, not in captions. The video points people to the
> *new schedule* without stating it. If you ever want the date back, it goes in as the
> optional token `{{SCHEDULE_LINE}}` and nowhere else.

---

### The scenario (one paragraph)

It's a normal Valuecart workday. Screens, dashboards, catalogs, order queues. Then a
notification pings across every desk at once — the **AI Agent Building Challenge** final
presentations have been rescheduled. Our mascot, the friendly white robot from the poster,
pops up as the in-video announcer and walks the team through what changed, what to prepare,
how they'll be judged, and the ₹2,00,000 + ₹2,00,000 on the table. It closes on the whole
team — tech and non-tech, side by side — walking into the presentation room. Tone:
**energetic internal hype, not a corporate memo.** A product-launch teaser aimed at our own people.

**Narrative arc in 40 seconds:** Attention → Correction → Clarity → Preparation → Reward → Rally.

---

### Video specs

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | **40 seconds** (hard cap; VO = 107 words @ ~150 wpm, voice speed 1.05x) |
| Primary aspect | 16:9 (1920×1080) — Teams / email / office screens |
| Secondary cut | 9:16 (1080×1920) — WhatsApp status + internal stories |
| Avatar | HeyGen presenter, business-casual, **lower-third right**, 30–35% frame height |
| Captions | Burned-in, bottom-center, bold sans, cyan keyword highlight |
| Music | Uplifting tech / future-corporate, 118–124 BPM, build at 0:26, resolve at 0:36 |
| Read style | Warm, upbeat, colleague-to-colleague. Not newsreader. |

### Locked facts (use these exact words on screen)

- Event: **AI Agent Building Challenge**, presented by **Valuecart**
- Status: **Final presentation schedule has been postponed** — this video is the notice
- Where to look: **"New presentation schedule is out"** (no date spoken or shown)
- Mode: **Presenting to all team members**
- **Both Tech & Non-Tech teams present on the same day**
- Prepare: your AI tool + its purpose · time reduction · cost savings · innovative AI methods
- Evaluation criteria: **1. Reduction in time · 2. Cost savings · 3. AI methods & innovation**
- Prizes: **₹2,00,000 cash — Tech team** and **₹2,00,000 cash — Non-Tech team**
- Mandatory: **all participating team members must present their AI tool**
- Sign-off: **"Let's showcase our innovations and shape our future together!"**
