# Valuecart · Independence Day — 40-Second Video
## Part 1 · Scenario + Creative Brief

> **Content rule: NO DATES.** No day, month, or year spoken, shown, or generated — consistent
> with the AI Challenge video. "Independence Day" identifies itself; it needs no date.

---

### The scenario (one paragraph)

One morning a year, the Valuecart office changes colour. Tricolour buntings across the floors,
saffron-white-green light spilling through the glass, the flag going up in the forecourt while
every team — ops, catalog, finance, tech, warehouse — stands still in one line. The film opens
on that stillness, opens up into the celebration (anthem, sweets, songs, group photo), then
turns the meaning outward: at a company whose whole job is taking brands into India and Indian
commerce to the world, **independence isn't only history — it's the freedom to build.** Closes
on the tricolour dissolving into the Valuecart logo. Tone: **proud, warm, cinematic. Restrained,
never loud.** Goosebumps, not fireworks.

**Narrative arc in 40 seconds:** Stillness → Unity → Celebration → Togetherness → Meaning → Wish.

### Video specs

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | **40 seconds** (VO = 99 words @ ~150 wpm, voice speed 1.0x — this one breathes) |
| Primary aspect | 16:9 (1920×1080) — office screens, Teams, email |
| Secondary cut | 9:16 (1080×1920) — WhatsApp status, stories |
| Avatar | **Bookends only** — scenes 1 and 6. Middle is pure B-roll + VO. |
| Captions | Burned-in, bottom-center, elegant serif or clean sans, white with saffron highlight |
| Music | Solo flute/bansuri open → strings build at 0:18 → full swell at 0:26 → resolve at 0:36. 70–80 BPM. |
| Read style | Warm, slow, sincere. Half a beat slower than normal. **Not** a news anchor, **not** hype. |

### Palette — national colours fused with Valuecart brand

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Saffron | `#FF9933` | Primary warmth, light leaks, top band |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Middle band, type, negative space |
| India Green | `#138808` | Lower band, foliage, accents |
| Chakra Navy | `#000080` | The Ashoka Chakra, fine linework |
| Valuecart Cyan | `#22D3EE` | Brand tie-in — endcard and logo only |
| Deep Indigo | `#0A0B2E` | Endcard base, matching the AI Challenge film |

**Visual DNA:** golden-hour natural light, real fabric, shallow depth of field, slow motion,
lens flares through tricolour cloth, dust motes in sunbeams. Where the AI Challenge film was
neon and synthetic, **this one is warm, physical and human** — the only synthetic element is
the final logo lockup, which is how the two films visibly belong to the same brand.

### Rules for this film

- **Show respect for the flag.** It is never on the ground, never used as clothing or drapery,
  never distorted, never cropped mid-hoist. Follow the Flag Code — hoisted briskly, saffron up.
- **No political imagery, no party colours, no military hardware, no leader portraits.**
- **Faces:** use real Valuecart footage if you have it. If generating, keep faces soft-focus,
  backlit or at three-quarter angle — never a sharp AI-generated "employee" who doesn't exist.
- **Ashoka Chakra:** 24 spokes, navy, correct proportions. Get this wrong and people notice.
- No dates, no year counts ("77th", "78th" etc. are date references — leave them out).
