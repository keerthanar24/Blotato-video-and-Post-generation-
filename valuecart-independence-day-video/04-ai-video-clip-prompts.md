### Generation settings that matter

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Motion strength | **Low** | Dignity reads as stillness; heavy motion cheapens this film |
| Slow motion | 40–50% on CLIP 02, 06, 11 | The unfurl is the emotional beat — let it land |
| Camera | One move per clip | Push-in **or** dolly, never both |
| Grade | Warm, +200K over neutral | Golden-hour consistency across every cut |
| Negative prompt | `text, watermark, logo, letters, numbers, dates, subtitles, distorted faces, extra fingers, deformed flag, flag touching ground, soldiers, weapons, politicians, religious symbols, incorrect chakra spokes` | Protects both the look and the respect rules |
| Center third | Keep clear on CLIP 08, 09, 11 | That's where the titles land |
