Transition out: a smooth cross-dissolve through a soft cyan light bloom.
VOICEOVER: "Shoppers now meet brands in feeds, reels, reviews, recommendations and category pages — long before anyone types a word."

[0:18-0:26] SCENE 4 - THE RECEIPT. This is the key visual of the film. [0:36-0:45] SCENE 6 - THE CLOSE. A clean brand frame: a glowing line-art map of India with soft luminous light trails arcing inward toward it from points around a faint globe grid.
