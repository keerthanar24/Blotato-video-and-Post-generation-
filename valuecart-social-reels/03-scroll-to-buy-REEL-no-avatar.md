# "SCROLL-TO-BUY" — Reel / TikTok Cut
### 35 seconds · 9:16 · NO AVATAR · voiceover + kinetic text + footage

This is the native-feed version: no presenter, no talking head, no corporate frame.
Built the way reels that actually travel are built — hook in the first second, text carrying
the message, a cut every 1.5–2.5 seconds, and an ending that loops back into the opening.

---

## The rules this cut follows

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **No logo until 0:33** | A logo in the first 3 seconds is the #1 scroll trigger. Brand at the end, once you've earned it |
| **Text is the star, not the voice** | Most people watch muted. If the reel doesn't work with sound off, it doesn't work |
| **Max 2.5s per shot** | The moment a shot outstays that, the thumb moves |
| **Second person, present tense** | "your brand", "you get found" — not "brands are discovering" |
| **Short sentences. Fragments allowed.** | This is spoken-internet cadence, not a press release |
| **Ends where it starts** | The last shot returns to the scroll, so a replay feels seamless |
| **No stock-corporate footage** | No handshakes, no boardrooms, no people pointing at laptops. Phone screens and real hands only |

## SHOT LIST

### `0:00 – 0:03` · HOOK — the scroll
**Visual:** Straight into a phone screen filling the frame. A shopping feed rips upward — product tiles, a reel, a review, an ad — heavy motion blur. A real thumb flicks up twice.
**Text on screen:** `NOBODY IS SEARCHING` / `FOR YOUR BRAND IN INDIA` *(big, centered, lands word by word on the beat)*
**Motion:** Feed at 3× speed. Text punches in at 1.15 scale and settles.
**Cut:** **Scroll-push up** — the feed carries the next shot in
**VO:** *"Nobody's searching for your brand in India. They're scrolling past it."*
**SFX:** Swipe whoosh ×2

### `0:03 – 0:08` · THEN vs NOW
**Visual:** Two fast shots. **(1)** A clean search bar, cursor blinking, everything still and slightly desaturated — feels dated on purpose. **(2)** Whip to a thumb scrolling a live feed, warm and fast.
**Text on screen:** `10 YEARS AGO IT STARTED HERE` → `NOW IT STARTS HERE`
**Motion:** Shot 1 held dead still for 1.5s. Shot 2 explodes into motion — the contrast *is* the edit.
**Cut:** **Whip-pan** between the two, 5-frame blur
**VO:** *"Ten years ago, buying started here. Now it starts here."*
**SFX:** Single keyboard click on shot 1. Whoosh into shot 2

### `0:08 – 0:14` · THE SIX SURFACES
**Visual:** Six rapid-fire shots, ~0.9s each, all phone-screen POV: a marketplace feed → a short video → a creator holding a product to camera → a sponsored ad slot → a 5-star review block → a category page.
**Text on screen:** One word snaps on per shot: `FEED` `REEL` `CREATOR` `AD` `REVIEWS` `CATEGORY`
**Motion:** Each word arrives with a hard scale-punch, cyan. Small zoom-in on every shot so nothing sits static.
**Cut:** **Zoom-punch match cuts** — each shot pushes into the next through the screen
**VO:** *"Feed. Reel. Creator. Review. Ad. Category page. That's where Indian shoppers meet brands now."*
**SFX:** A click per word. Riser building underneath

### `0:14 – 0:21` · THE TURN ⚡ *(the whole reel exists for this)*
**Visual:** Everything **stops.** A single search bar centered on black. A hand types a brand name — slowly, deliberately. Then a line draws under it: `SEE → COMPARE → REVISIT → SEARCH → BUY`, with `SEARCH` almost at the very end. A cyan bracket sweeps the first three: `DECISION HAPPENS HERE`. Then a stamp lands on `SEARCH`: **RECEIPT.**
**Text on screen:** `SEARCH ISN'T DISCOVERY` / **`IT'S THE RECEIPT`** *(largest text in the reel)*
**Motion:** Hard stop on all motion for 8 frames before the typing starts. Timeline draws in 1.2s. The `RECEIPT` stamp lands on the beat drop.
**Cut:** **Light-sweep** out
**VO:** *"So by the time someone actually types your name — they've already decided. Search isn't discovery anymore. It's the receipt."*
**SFX:** Silence → typing → **beat drop on "receipt"**

### `0:21 – 0:29` · WHAT THAT MEANS FOR YOU
**Visual:** Four punchy shots cut to the beat, each a phone screen zoomed right in: product listing images → a review scroll → a sponsored placement → a price tag.
**Text on screen:** Four full-screen statements, one per shot:
`YOUR IMAGES = YOUR STOREFRONT`
`YOUR REVIEWS = YOUR SALES PITCH`
`YOUR ADS = YOUR SHELF`
`YOUR PRICE = FIRST IMPRESSION`
**Motion:** Each statement slams in on the downbeat, cyan on the second half of each line. Slight camera shake on impact — subtle, 2px.
**Cut:** **Hard cuts on the beat.** No transitions here — the rhythm is the transition
**VO:** *"So your listing images are your storefront. Your reviews are the sales pitch. Your ads are the shelf. Your price is the first impression."*
**SFX:** Beat-locked. No extra SFX — let the music carry it

### `0:29 – 0:35` · CLOSE + LOOP
**Visual:** Back to the scrolling feed from shot 1 — **same footage, now moving slowly** instead of racing. The thumb pauses on one product. Holds. Then over the final line the Valuecart logo fades up small, bottom-center. Last frame: the feed still gently scrolling.
**Text on screen:** `GET DISCOVERED BEFORE YOU GET SEARCHED` <br> then → `Valuecart` *(small, understated)*
**Motion:** Feed at 0.3× speed. Logo fades up over 0.8s — no animation, no swoosh, no sting. The restraint is what makes it land.
**Cut:** **No ending card. It loops** — the final frame matches the opening frame so a replay is seamless
**VO:** *"India's going scroll-first. Get discovered before you get searched. Or don't get found at all."*
**SFX:** Music drops out on the last word, leaving one soft scroll sound
