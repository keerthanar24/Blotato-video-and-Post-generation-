# Valuecart Reel · "SCROLL-TO-BUY"
### 45 seconds · 9:16 · LinkedIn + Instagram + YouTube Shorts

Source: the Valuecart thought-leadership post *"India Is Moving From Search-to-Buy to Scroll-to-Buy."*

---

## The idea, in one line

**The search bar is no longer where buying starts — it's where buying gets confirmed.**

Everything in this reel is evidence for that one thought. The line that carries it — and the
line people will repeat — is: *"By the time they search, they've already decided. The search is
just the receipt."*

---

## Why it's built this way

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Hook is a threat, not a greeting | *"Your next customer in India won't search for you."* No logo, no "in today's world" |
| Branded stinger runs **under** the hook, not before it | A logo in the first second costs you ~30% of viewers on LinkedIn and IG autoplay |
| Captions burned in, always | 80%+ of LinkedIn feed views are silent. The reel has to work with sound off |
| Transitions are **smooth, never glitchy** | Scroll-pushes and liquid morphs — the film should *feel* like scrolling |
| One idea only | Search vs. scroll. The six capability words are evidence, not topics |
| Ends on their own question | *"Ready to be discovered before you're searched?"* — straight from the post |

## Transition map

| Cut | At | Transition | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 → S2 | 0:04 | Scroll-push up | The film moves the way a feed moves |
| S2 → S3 | 0:10 | Liquid morph | Search *becoming* scroll, not replaced by it |
| S3 → S4 | 0:18 | Cross-dissolve through bloom | Softest cut in the reel — lets the idea land |
| S4 → S5 | 0:26 | Diagonal light-sweep | Insight → action |
| S5 → S6 | 0:36 | Parallax push-through | Tactics → brand |

**All smooth. No glitch, no RGB split, no hard flash** — this reel is a considered argument,
and jittery transitions would undercut the authority the voice is building.
