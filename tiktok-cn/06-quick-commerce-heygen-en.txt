Create a 40-second vertical 9:16 short video (1080x1920) for TikTok, aimed at cross-border e-commerce sellers and global brands entering India.

VOICE: English, female, calm, warm and restrained. Low-key and trustworthy, NOT an energetic sales or shopping-channel tone. Speed 0.95x. The first four scenes are almost wordless - let the cutting carry them.

STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic. Shallow depth of field, natural light, fine film grain, anamorphic bokeh. Warm, contemporary, urban India. No avatar and no presenter on screen - voiceover only over footage. Burned-in captions, all caps, bold sans-serif, 2-4 words per card, placed in the middle-lower third, clear of the TikTok interface.

NO SINGLE PRODUCT: Show many categories and many plain unbranded parcels. Never feature one specific product or brand.

CORE VISUAL IDEA - THE GAP THAT DISAPPEARS: Two shots recur throughout the opening - a thumb tapping a phone, and a hand receiving a bag at a door. They are always framed identically. Each time they repeat, the gap between them is shorter, until in Scene 4 there is no cut between them at all.

SIGNATURE TRANSITION: Accelerating match cuts. Scenes 1 to 3 use the SAME two framings each time, cut tighter on every repetition. Scene 4 replaces the cut with one continuous whip pan - the gap is gone. From Scene 5 onward the film opens out and settles into ordinary, unhurried cutting, which should feel like a release after the compression.

Build exactly 11 scenes. Use the voiceover lines VERBATIM.

Voiceover: "Watch how short this gets."
Caption: WATCH THE GAP.
Visual: Macro on a thumb tapping a phone screen. Hard match cut to a hand receiving a paper bag at an apartment door. Two shots, identical energy and framing. Warm afternoon light.

SCENE 3 - 3.0s
Voiceover: "Order. Doorbell."
Caption: FASTER.

Caption: ALMOST / NO GAP.

Caption: QUICK COMMERCE.
