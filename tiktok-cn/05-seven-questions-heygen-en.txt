Create a 40-second vertical 9:16 short video (1080x1920) for TikTok, aimed at cross-border e-commerce sellers and global brands considering India.

VOICE: English, female, calm, warm and restrained. Low-key and trustworthy, NOT an energetic sales or shopping-channel tone. Speed 0.95x. Let the pauses between scenes breathe.

STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic. Shallow depth of field, fine film grain, anamorphic bokeh. The film begins cold, indirect and blue-grey, and ends fully warm and golden. No avatar and no presenter on screen - voiceover only over footage. Burned-in captions, all caps, bold sans-serif, 2-4 words per card, placed in the middle-lower third, clear of the TikTok interface.

NO SINGLE PRODUCT: The subject is one plain shrink-wrapped pallet of unbranded brown cartons. Never show a specific product or brand.

CORE VISUAL IDEA - THE THING THAT NEVER MOVES: One pallet stands perfectly still and razor sharp while the entire world around it runs as streaked long-exposure motion. Above it hang seven pendant lamps, all dark. Each question answered lights one lamp. Only when all seven are lit does the pallet finally move.

SIGNATURE TRANSITION: Speed is the transition. The world runs at high-speed time-lapse with light-trail motion blur while the pallet stays locked at normal speed and in perfect focus. The camera moves in one slow continuous orbit around the pallet from Scene 4 to Scene 8. There are no whip cuts and no wipes in this film - it is built on stillness, and the only real movement is saved for Scene 10.

Build exactly 11 scenes. Use the voiceover lines VERBATIM.

SCENE 1 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "This shipment has been ready for months."
Caption: READY. / STILL WAITING.
Visual: A single shrink-wrapped pallet of plain brown cartons, razor sharp and completely still, in a warehouse where everything else is streaked long-exposure motion - forklifts as light trails, people as soft blurs. The one still thing in a moving world. Cold blue-grey light.

SCENE 2 - 3.0s
Voiceover: "Nothing is wrong with the demand."
Caption: DEMAND / ISN'T THE PROBLEM.

SCENE 3 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "The brand isn't hesitating about India."
Caption: NOT HESITATING / ABOUT INDIA.
Visual: The time-lapse slows down to normal speed. The blurred world resolves into focus around the still pallet. Everything goes quiet.
Transition: A speed ramp, not a cut. The world decelerates into stillness.

SCENE 4 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "It's hesitating about seven questions."
Caption: SEVEN QUESTIONS.
Visual: The camera begins a slow, continuous orbit around the pallet. Above it, seven pendant lamps hang in a wide ring - every one of them dark. The only light in the space is cold and indirect.

SCENE 5 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "Who handles compliance? How does customs clear?"
Caption: COMPLIANCE / CUSTOMS
Visual: The orbit continues. One lamp flicks on, then a second, dropping two warm pools of light onto the pallet. Brief macro cutaways: a stamp pressing down, a seal - both soft-focus and unreadable.

SCENE 6 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "Which marketplace first? What's the landed cost?"
Caption: MARKETPLACE / LANDED COST

SCENE 7 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "Where does inventory sit? Who handles returns and payments?"
Caption: INVENTORY / RETURNS / PAYMENTS

SCENE 8 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "And who owns it, every single day?"
Caption: WHO OWNS IT / EVERY DAY?
Visual: The orbit stops dead. One lamp is still dark - the seventh, hanging directly above the pallet. Hold on it. The hardest question is the one still unanswered. Total stillness.

SCENE 9 - 3.5s
Voiceover: "These aren't reasons to avoid India."
Caption: NOT REASONS / TO AVOID.
Visual: The seventh lamp flickers once, then comes on strong. The full ring is lit. The pallet is completely, warmly illuminated for the first time in the film.

Caption: ENTER WITH / A STRUCTURE.

Caption: INDIA IS READY. / ARE YOU?
