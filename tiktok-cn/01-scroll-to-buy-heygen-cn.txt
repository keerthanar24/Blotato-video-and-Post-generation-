STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic B-roll of modern India. Shallow depth of field, natural light, fine film grain, anamorphic bokeh. Warm, vibrant, premium documentary look. NO SINGLE PRODUCT: Never feature one specific hero product. Show many different people, many different categories, many different Indian settings. The subject is the behaviour, not an item.
