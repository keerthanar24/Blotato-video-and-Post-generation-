Search Is Where It Ends

A 30-second vertical Reel that follows one shopper and one bottle through a discovery-led purchase — and lands the search bar exactly where it now belongs: last.

0:30 runtime 9:16 · 1080×1920 8 scenes 58 spoken words Voiceover-led Burned-in captions

Build settings

Set these before you paste the script

Format
Voiceover over B-roll — no avatar on screen

The brief asks for photorealistic cinematic footage. An avatar in frame competes with it. If you need a face for brand trust, put one in scene 8 only.

Voice
Indian English, female, conversational

Speed ~0.95×. Pick warmth over polish — this is a story about one person, not an announcement.

Pacing
One HeyGen scene per row below

Eight separate scenes, not one continuous read. The scene breaks buy the ~7 seconds of silence that make 58 words fill 30.

Captions
On, burned in, all caps

Heavy sans (Montserrat ExtraBold or Archivo Black), white on a soft dark plate, 2–4 words per card.

Safe area
Captions between 20% and 75% frame height

Clears the Reels top bar, the bottom caption strip, and the right-hand icon column.

Continuity
One woman, one amber bottle, all 8 scenes

That repetition is the argument. If the product changes between scenes, the story stops meaning anything.

Watch-outs

Where this one breaks

01

Never let generated B-roll render readable text. Search bars, review counts and price tags come back as convincing gibberish. Keep every screen soft-focus and let your burned-in captions carry the words — that is also why the captions are doing real narrative work here, not just accessibility.

02

Scenes 5 and 6 are one sentence split across a cut. The ellipses matter. If the voice lands a full stop at the end of scene 5, the reveal in scene 6 stops being a reveal.

03

The brand speaks once. Everything before 0:24 is the shopper's story. Moving the Valuecart message earlier turns a story into an ad and costs you the retention.

04

Check the hook on mute. Most people meet scene 1 with no sound. The freeze on the bottle at ~0:02 has to read as a surprise on its own.
