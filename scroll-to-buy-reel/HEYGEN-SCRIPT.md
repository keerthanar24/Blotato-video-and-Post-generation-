# Search Is Where It Ends — HeyGen production script

**Valuecart · Reel 04 · "India Is Moving From Search-to-Buy to Scroll-to-Buy"**

0:30 · 9:16 (1080×1920) · 8 scenes · 58 spoken words · voiceover-led · burned-in captions

---

## Build settings

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Voiceover over B-roll — no avatar on screen. If you need a face for brand trust, put one in scene 8 only. |
| **Voice** | Indian English, female, conversational. Speed ~0.95×. Warmth over polish. |
| **Pacing** | One HeyGen scene per row below — 8 separate scenes, not one continuous read. The scene breaks buy the ~7s of silence that make 58 words fill 30. |
| **Captions** | On, burned in, all caps. Heavy sans (Montserrat ExtraBold / Archivo Black), white on a soft dark plate, 2–4 words per card. |
| **Safe area** | Captions between 20% and 75% of frame height — clears the Reels top bar, bottom caption strip, and right-hand icon column. |
| **Continuity** | One woman, one amber bottle, all 8 scenes. The repetition *is* the argument. |

## Scene by scene

Colour arc: cold screen-glow → warm daylight.

### 1 · 0:00 · 3.0s — HOOK
**VO:** She never searched for this brand.
**Caption:** SHE NEVER / SEARCHED.
**Visual:** Extreme macro on a young Indian woman's eye in a dark bedroom, lit only by phone glow. A feed races across her iris as a blur of light — then stops dead on a single amber glass serum bottle. Anamorphic bokeh, fine grain, slow push-in.

### 2 · 0:03 · 2.5s — SETUP
**VO:** She just scrolled.
**Caption:** SHE JUST / SCROLLED.
**Visual:** Thumb-scroll POV, vertical. The feed smears upward into a river of light. Hyper-speed, heavy motion blur, cold screen cast on the fingertip.

### 3 · 0:05.5 · 4.5s — THE THREE TOUCHES
**VO:** A reel. A review. A price she half-remembered.
**Caption:** A REEL. / A REVIEW. / A PRICE.
**Visual:** Three hard cuts, ~1.5s each, on the beat of the line: a short-video thumbnail of the bottle; a wall of five-star ratings; a price on a category page. Macro on glass, shallow focus throughout.

### 4 · 0:10 · 5.0s — REPETITION
**VO:** Day after day, the feed kept handing her the same bottle.
**Caption:** THE SAME BOTTLE / AGAIN AND AGAIN
**Visual:** The same woman in three light states — golden morning through curtains, flat afternoon on a commuter train, blue night in bed. The same amber bottle passes on screen each time. Days compressing.

### 5 · 0:15 · 4.0s — TURN
**VO:** And when she finally opened the search bar…
**Caption:** THEN SHE / SEARCHED.
**Visual:** Extreme macro. A thumb taps. A cursor blinks in an empty field. Letters begin to appear — decisive, no hesitation. Warm light starts creeping in from the left.

### 6 · 0:19 · 2.5s — REVEAL
**VO:** …she wasn't discovering anything.
**Caption:** SHE WASN'T / DISCOVERING.
**Visual:** Hold on the field. Autocomplete has already filled in the exact brand name. Slight slow-motion on the blink of the cursor.

### 7 · 0:21.5 · 2.5s — PAYOFF
**VO:** She was confirming it.
**Caption:** SHE WAS / CONFIRMING.
**Visual:** Cut to a parcel on a sunlit apartment floor in India. Hands lift the flap; the amber bottle catches gold light. Dust in the air, window-grille shadows across the floor.

### 8 · 0:24 · 6.0s — THE INSIGHT
**VO:** In India, search isn't where buying begins. It's where it ends.
**Caption:** SEARCH ISN'T WHERE / BUYING BEGINS. / IT'S WHERE IT ENDS.
**Visual:** The bottle settles in her hand in warm daylight. The push-in slows and holds. End card fades up over the final 3 seconds.

## Watch-outs

1. **Never let generated B-roll render readable text.** Search bars, review counts and price tags come back as convincing gibberish. Keep every screen soft-focus and let the burned-in captions carry the words — which is why the captions here do real narrative work, not just accessibility.
2. **Scenes 5 and 6 are one sentence split across a cut.** The ellipses matter. If the voice lands a full stop at the end of scene 5, the reveal in scene 6 stops being a reveal.
3. **The brand speaks once.** Everything before 0:24 is the shopper's story. Moving the Valuecart message earlier turns a story into an ad and costs retention.
4. **Check the hook on mute.** Most people meet scene 1 with no sound. The freeze on the bottle at ~0:02 has to read as a surprise on its own.
