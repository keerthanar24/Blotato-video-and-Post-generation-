# Reel — "Scroll-to-Buy" (Valuecart)

**Format:** 30s · 9:16 · photorealistic cinematic · VO + burned-in captions
**Thesis:** In India, purchase intent is created in the feed. The search bar is the last step, not the first.
**Story spine:** one shopper, one product (an amber glass serum bottle from a global brand entering India).

## Structure

| # | Time | Beat | Visual |
|---|------|------|--------|
| 1 | 0–8s  | HOOK — speed then sudden stillness | Feed racing as a river of light across her eye; everything stops on one bottle |
| 2 | 8–16s | THE DRIFT — the product keeps coming back | Same bottle recurring: reel, 5-star reviews, category shelf; day/night light shifts |
| 3 | 16–24s | THE SEARCH — arrives LAST | Macro: thumb types the exact brand name; autocomplete already knows it |
| 4 | 24–30s | THE PAYOFF — insight | Parcel opens in warm Indian daylight, bottle in hand |

## Voiceover (64 words · ~30s at reel pace)

1. She never searched for this brand.
2. She just scrolled.
3. A reel. A review. A price she half-remembered.
4. Day after day, the feed kept handing her the same bottle.
5. And when she finally opened the search bar —
6. she wasn't discovering anything.
7. She was confirming it.
8. In India, search is no longer where buying begins. It's where it ends.

## Burned-in captions
Same as VO, 2–4 words per card, bottom third, safe-area clear of Reels UI.

## End card
VALUECART — marketplace execution for global brands entering India.
